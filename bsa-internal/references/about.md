# About BSA

## Identity

- Name: Blockchain Student Association (BSA), EPFL.
- A student-led association for blockchain at EPFL, in Lausanne, Switzerland.
- Mission: foster blockchain education, innovation and community among students interested in decentralized technologies. BSA connects students, founders and industry through workshops, conferences, hackathons and joint projects.
- Structure: a flat hierarchy with a rotating executive committee. BSA is **not a DAO**. Never describe it as one.

## History

- 14 September 2018: constitutive general assembly.
- 2018-2019: first year of events: "Genesis" introduction (4 Oct 2018), blockchain introduction, "Create your own dApps" and "Create your own blockchain" workshops, a lecture on cryptoeconomics, and talks on crypto assets and on blockchain gaming.
- Early research and development with the EPFL labs DEDIS and DCL (for example tools for the DEDIS Cothority network).
- 2021: official recognition by EPFL.
- October 2023: first Sui x BSA Hackathon, the first European Sui hackathon.
- March 2024: first BSA conference, the EPFL Blockchain Conference.
- From 2025: a spring conference with a hackathon on the same theme (Privacy & Verifiability in 2025, Stablecoins & Payments in 2026), and an autumn hackathon.

The website says BSA is among the first blockchain student associations in Switzerland. Its exact wording is ambiguous ("the first blockchain student associations"), so do not claim "the first" without checking.

## Activities

- Conferences: talks and panels on privacy, DeFi, stablecoins and regulation.
- Hackathons: 30 to 36 hours, prize pools from about $5.7k to $25k, open to students from any university.
- Education: workshops for beginners and advanced members, and the education platform [edu.bsaepfl.ch](https://edu.bsaepfl.ch) (course: zkSNARKs 101).
- Research: published articles (see `startups-research.md`). Members have contributed to the EPFL labs DEDIS, DCL and COMPSEC.
- Startups: support for projects started by members (see `startups-research.md`).
- Partnerships: sponsorships, co-organized events, research and recruiting.

## Teams

Research, Education, Communication, Logistics, IT, Sponsorship, and Industry, plus the executive committee.

## Members

- Membership is free. Most events are free.
- Any EPFL student can join. No blockchain experience is needed.
- Members come from EPFL, ETH Zurich, HEC Lausanne (UNIL) and HEIG-VD.

## Reach (figures from the website, 2026)

- 350+ conference attendees (363 at the Privacy & Verifiability Conference, March 2025).
- 200 hackathon participants (Sui x BSA, September 2025, and The Hackathon, October 2026).
- 20+ universities represented at hackathons on average.
- $90k+ in hackathon prizes in total.
- Active since 2018.
