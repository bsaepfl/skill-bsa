## What BSA Offers Partners

- Formats: hackathon sponsorships and tracks, conference partnerships, technical workshops and bounties, recruiting sessions, co-organized events, research collaboration, longer-term ecosystem partnerships.
- Packages: event-specific or multi-event, adapted to the partner's goals. The sponsorship team sends the current one-pager on request.
- Benefits, depending on the partnership:
  - Visibility on stage and online, and branded presence at events.
  - Direct access to EPFL blockchain talent for recruiting, through talks, workshops and introductions.
  - Running technical workshops or bounties.
  - Inclusion in BSA's community communications.
  - Access to EPFL research faculty and the Zurich-Lausanne network.
- Reach: see "Reach" in `about.md`.
- Many members went on to work at partner protocols and research groups.
- BSA often co-organizes events with peer student associations in Europe and in the Zurich-Lausanne area.
- First contact: bsa@epfl.ch, with a short note about the company and its goals.
