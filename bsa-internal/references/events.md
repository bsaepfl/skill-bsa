# Past Events

All events took place at EPFL, Lausanne. Since 2025 they use the BC Building. Most recent first.

## The Hackathon (October 2026)

- Type: hackathon. Dates: 10-11 October 2026.
- 200 participants, 49 projects, $5,700 prize pool.
- Sponsors and tracks: Belem Capital and Swissquote (one track each).
- Free food for participants. Registration on Luma.
- Event website: [hackathon.bsaepfl.ch](https://hackathon.bsaepfl.ch)

## Stablecoins & Payments Hackathon (March 2026)

- Type: hackathon, 36 hours. BSA's flagship spring hackathon.
- 160 participants from across Europe, 55 projects, $16k+ prize pool.
- Sponsors: AlphaTON Capital, XRPL Commons, ENS, Ledger.
- Tracks: stablecoin issuance, on/off-ramps, payments UX, on-chain compliance.
- Mentorship from partner protocols during the event. Final teams pitched on the conference main stage.
- Starter template: x402 payments on TON with BSA USD, a testnet stablecoin made by BSA ([bsa-sp-template-x402-2026](https://github.com/bsaepfl/bsa-sp-template-x402-2026)).

## Stablecoins & Payments Conference (March 2026)

- Type: conference. 3 stages, 20+ speakers. Tracks: Policy, Tech, Markets.
- Speakers from Circle, Swiss National Bank, Aave, Franklin Templeton, Ledger and UNHCR.
- Topics: CBDC design, MiCA implementation, on-chain settlement, RWA tokenization, stablecoins for aid distribution.
- Sponsors: AlphaTON Capital, Ledger, XRPL Commons, ENS, Hyli, Raiffeisen, Swissquote, Taurus, SYZ, Supernova, MiCA Crypto Alliance, CVA, WiW3CH, CMTA.

## Sui x BSA Hackathon, 3rd edition (September 2025)

- Type: hackathon. Described as Europe's biggest student-run Sui hackathon.
- 200 participants, teams of up to 4, $20k+ prize pool.
- Tracks: DeFi, gaming, consumer.
- Co-host and sponsor: Sui Foundation. Sui Foundation engineers were on site for the whole event.
- Pre-hackathon bootcamp: 3 workshops on Move, the Sui object model, and advanced features (zkLogin, sponsored transactions).
- Starter template: [bsa-sui-template-frontend-2025](https://github.com/bsaepfl/bsa-sui-template-frontend-2025) (Sui, Walrus, Seal).

## Privacy & Verifiability Hackathon (March 2025)

- Type: hackathon, 36 hours.
- 36 hackers, 23 projects, $15k prize pool.
- Tracks: zero-knowledge proofs (ZKP), multi-party computation (MPC), trusted execution environments (TEE).
- Sponsors and judges: Mina Foundation, Hedera Hashgraph Association, Hyli.
- Held at the same time as the conference, so hackers could attend the talks.

## Privacy & Verifiability Conference (March 2025)
