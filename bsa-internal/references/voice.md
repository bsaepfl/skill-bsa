# Brand Voice

From BSA's website design brief.

## Voice

- Three words: **cypherpunk, quietly prolific, detail-obsessed.**
- Serious people doing real work. A real community. Cutting-edge research.
- Cypherpunk heritage (privacy, autonomy, open systems) is a quiet base, not a slogan.

## Rules

1. **Show, do not explain.** Lead with results: events, participants, prizes, startups, research. Internal processes come last or not at all.
2. **Give figures with dates and sources** from the other references.
3. **One voice for students and sponsors.** The same text must invite a student and convince a sponsor.
4. **Never say BSA is a DAO.** It has a flat hierarchy with an executive committee.
5. **Avoid:** party tone, corporate or consulting-firm language, hype, and "we are a DAO run by students" explanations.

## Visual Identity

- Primary color: BSA Blue `#152237`. Do not change it.
- Display font: Instrument Serif, for large headings.
- The website uses dark mode only.

## Channels

From BSA's communication guidelines of 2019. Confirm with the Communication team that they still apply.

- X: formal language. Announce events, share articles and research.
- Instagram: not familiar, not too formal. Photos of events and of members at work, short series.
- Events: announce 5 to 7 days before, and up to 2 weeks before for large events. Post a reminder 2 days before.
