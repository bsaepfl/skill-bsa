# BSA Internal Knowledge

This skill is for BSA members who write about the association, mainly in communication and sponsorship.

1. For mission, history, teams, contacts and reach figures: read `references/about.md`.
2. For past events and their figures: read `references/events.md`.
3. For prizes won by members: read `references/wins.md`.
4. For sponsors, partners and what sponsors get: read `references/partners.md`.
5. For startups, research articles and education: read `references/startups-research.md`.
6. Before writing any text for the public, a sponsor or a partner: read `references/voice.md`.

## Rules

- Use only facts from these references. Never invent figures, dates, names, prizes or partners.
- If a fact is not in the references, say so and ask the user. Do not fill the gap.
- Sponsorship prices and packages are not in these references. Say that the sponsorship team sends the current one-pager on request.
- When a figure has a date, keep the date: "200 participants in October 2026", not "200 participants".
- This repository is public. Never add private data to it: personal phone numbers or emails, internal links, finances, or contract terms.
